# CIVICPULSE — NON-NEGOTIABLE PRODUCT NORTH STAR

## Primary Purpose
> "CivicPulse aggregates citizen development requests via voice, text, and messaging apps across diverse linguistic regions of India. The system analyses large datasets combining citizen feedback with national demographic data, infrastructure indices, and public investment plans, surfacing demand hotspots and recommending high-priority development projects to national policymakers."

---

## Core Decision Pipeline
1. **Citizen Input**: Multilingual voice, text, and messaging submissions.
2. **Multilingual Understanding**: Preserves original language and extracts structured representation (category, issue, location, language, severity, urgency).
3. **Aggregation of Citizen Requests**: Translates individual signals into community problem clusters.
4. **Demand Hotspots**: Geospatial concentrations across State → District → City → Locality driven by actual data.
5. **Public Data Integration**: Combines citizen signals with demographics, infrastructure baselines, vulnerability indices, and public investment budgets.
6. **Development Need Measurement**: Deterministic, explainable scoring calculating where the need is greatest.
7. **Government Investment Alignment**: Evaluates whether public resources and Centrally Sponsored Schemes are aligned with identified demand.
8. **Actionable Recommendations**: High-priority development project briefs with full data lineage.
9. **Action Queue**: Tracking execution lifecycle across administrative departments.
10. **Impact Measurement**: Verifying real-world signal reduction and infrastructure improvements following project delivery.

---

## Data Lineage Mandate
Every recommendation MUST be traceable:
`Recommendation → Priority Score → Evidence → Public Investment → Infrastructure → Population/Vulnerability → Location → Community Issue → Citizen Signals`

---

## Prohibitions
- DO NOT turn CivicPulse into a generic chatbot or generic grievance ticketing system.
- DO NOT invent or hallucinate data, population metrics, budgets, or hotspots.
- DO NOT create disconnected dashboards, generic playgrounds, or ungrounded AI features.
- AI's role is strictly understanding, structuring, aggregating, and explaining verified evidence; deterministic data determines ranking and priority.

# CivicPulse

### AI-Powered Citizen Feedback → Policy Intelligence

CivicPulse is an AI-powered civic technology platform that transforms citizen development requests into structured, prioritized insights for infrastructure planning.

[🌐 Live Demo](YOUR_LINK) • [🎥 Demo Video](YOUR_LINK) • [📊 Presentation](YOUR_LINK)

## 🎯 Problem

Governments across India struggle to consolidate citizen feedback and align it with national infrastructure priorities.

Development requests are often fragmented across different channels, making it difficult to identify infrastructure gaps, understand demand hotspots, and prioritize development effectively.

## 💡 Solution

CivicPulse provides a unified AI-powered platform that collects citizen development requests through natural language and transforms them into structured insights.

The platform combines citizen feedback with demographic data, infrastructure indicators, and government investment priorities to help identify high-priority development areas.

## 🤖 AI & Technical Architecture

Gemini is used to understand natural-language citizen feedback and extract structured information such as location, category, and severity.

The resulting structured information is then processed by CivicPulse's application logic and combined with infrastructure and demographic indicators.

## 🎯 Priority Scoring

CivicPulse uses a weighted scoring model to evaluate development priorities.

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Citizen Demand | 30% |
| Infrastructure Gap | 25% |
| Population Impact | 20% |
| Urgency & Severity | 15% |
| Government Alignment | 10% |
